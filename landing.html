Everything you need

Built for serious business owners

Stop fighting your invoicing software. InvoiceMaster Pro does the heavy lifting so you can focus on your work.

🏢
Multi-Organization Workspaces
Manage multiple businesses from one account. Switch between organizations instantly, each with its own isolated data, branding, and settings.
🧮
Deterministic Calculations
Zero floating-point errors. Every total, tax, discount, and subtotal is calculated using safe integer arithmetic — guaranteed to match your PDF and print exports.
🔄
Quote-to-Invoice Conversion
Send a quote, get it accepted, convert it to an invoice with one click. All history, notes, and line items carry over perfectly.
🎨
Professional Templates
Choose from Classic Corporate, Minimal Clean, and Bold Studio templates. Customize your logo, brand colors, and footer notes for every document.
💰
Payment Tracking
Record partial and full payments. Track outstanding balances per customer. Support for bank transfer, Stripe, PayPal, and more.
📊
Client & Revenue Insights
See revenue by period, top clients, overdue invoices, and outstanding balances at a glance — right on your dashboard.
📄
PDF & Print Export
Export pixel-perfect PDFs or use the browser print dialog. All templates are print-optimized with precise page-break handling.
🌍
Multi-Currency & Tax
Support for 10+ currencies. Configure VAT, GST, Sales Tax, or custom tax types. Tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive modes per document.
🔐
Privacy-First, Local Data
Your data stays in your browser. Full backup & restore with JSON export. No forced cloud sync — you own your data completely.
